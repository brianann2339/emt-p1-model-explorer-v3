# Net benefit at the recorded research threshold

Net benefit weights false positives by the odds of the decision threshold. The same numeric probability threshold can imply very different clinical consequences for a rare outcome. These fixed-threshold P104 arm values are not benefit at the final display-model threshold; nor are they estimated causal treatment effects. For constant-threshold rows, the independent check is TP/N − FP/N × threshold/(1−threshold).
