# PROBAST+AI computational self-appraisal

The completed computational evidence review below separates development quality, evaluation risk of bias and applicability. It uses the four-domain structure described by the [official PROBAST+AI resource](https://www.probast.org/) and [publication](https://www.bmj.com/content/388/bmj-2024-082505). The publisher-hosted full tool was inaccessible in this run (HTTP 403); this table is a study-specific evidence mapping, not a completed official signalling-question form or a claim of independent certification. It does not turn a software validation PASS into low clinical risk of bias.

## Remaining risk judgments

This review does not assign an overall low-risk label. Analysis has a substantial rare-event concern, and evaluation/applicability retains historical exposure and single-centre limitations. A domain clinician and named methods assessor must complete the official tool using the actual source measurement, outcome ascertainment and intended deployment information; those observations cannot be inferred from validator counts. AUTHOR_DECLARATIONS.md lists the missing source-owner statements. These are not reasons to discard verified model checkpoints or rerun already-accepted work.
