# P1_04 methods and checklist

## Scope

Medicine and Surgery each retain the complete original P1_04 grid: 12 ablation families, city/age/grouped-CV/congestion sensitivities, P3 airway sensitivities, three DNR rules, and the declared missingness variants across P1–P3 and S1–S6. The corrected current time plan supplies 18 time items (nine per specialty); the historical old20 route remains secondary evidence only.

## Execution and reuse

Each logical task was assigned independently to one of ten private Kaggle accounts. The assignment set excluded the six local tasks that completed before cloud activation. Terminal outputs were captured once per kernel and validated from their task/result roots. Verified task frontiers were preserved; no successor or duplicate run was needed after the dedicated revalidation.

The unchanged accepted baseline/evaluation material was reused only where its task identity, source arm, split, fold, seed and payload bindings matched. Validation did not fit models, call prediction, rerun bootstrap, change selection, or read new raw 2025 data.
